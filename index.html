## about

Andreas Janson is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Information Systems and Digital Business (IWI-HSG), University of St.Gallen, where he leads projects funded by the SNSF and Innosuisse. He is also Head of Working Group and Acting Professor for Information Systems, Socio-Technical System Design (WISTS) at the University of Hamburg. He received his PhD in Information Systems from the University of Kassel with highest distinction (summa cum laude).

His research studies how digital services are understood and designed across domains, including digital learning, gamification and digital nudging. His work appears in journals such as ISR, JAIS, JIT, EJIS, ISJ, AMLE, Computers & Education and Computers in Human Behavior, and at ICIS, ECIS, DESRIST, HICSS and WI.
