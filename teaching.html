Every semester since 2013: Bachelor and Master courses at St.Gallen and Kassel, built on a flipped-classroom design that the Association for Information Systems recognised with its Award for Innovation in Teaching.

## teaching philosophy

Learning processes are re-thought by incorporating digital technologies where they improve outcomes. The core is the flipped classroom: students prepare with self-regulated digital material before the lecture, and the online phases also target higher-order learning goals through scaffolding. Students then arrive better prepared for the time spent together in the lecture hall.

Research on technology-mediated learning feeds directly into daily teaching practice. Certified in university didactics (LLukas, University of Kassel) certificate. The flipped-classroom team received the AIS Innovation in Teaching Award ↗.
