## teaching philosophy

Learning processes are re-thought by incorporating digital technologies where they improve outcomes. The core is the flipped classroom: students prepare with self-regulated digital material before the lecture, and the online phases also target higher-order learning goals through scaffolding. Students then arrive better prepared for the time spent together in the lecture hall.

Research on technology-mediated learning feeds directly into daily teaching practice. Certified in university didactics through LLukas, the 80-hour continuing education programme of the University of Kassel (2018–2020): fundamentals of teaching and learning, activating teaching methods, competence-oriented assessment, textbook planning and writing, electronic exams and a teaching portfolio. The flipped-classroom team received the AIS Innovation in Teaching Award ↗.
